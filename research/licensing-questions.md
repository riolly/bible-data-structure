# Licensing questions to send

Two letters that only a person can send. Each is ready to paste. Fill in the bracketed parts, send it, and record the answer under "Answers" with its date. An answer that changes a limit also changes the edition's rights record in `src/contract/rights.ts`.

The facts behind each question are in [licensing.md](licensing.md), "Closed-license translations".

## To API.Bible

Send through the Contact link on [care.api.bible](https://care.api.bible/), API.Bible's help site, and name the account that owns the API key.

> Subject: Three questions about the terms, for a free Bible reading app
>
> Hello,
>
> We are [organization], a registered organization. We are building a free, non-commercial Bible reading app for English readers. It reads the NASB 1995, the NLT and the NIV through API.Bible on the Starter plan. Before we release it we want to be sure that three things are within your terms.
>
> 1. Converting the JSON for display. We request chapters with `content-type=json` and convert that JSON into our own chapter format, which our app draws on screen. We keep every word, the punctuation, the capitalization, the footnotes and the copyright line you send, and we change no text. Does this conversion count as a change to "any part of the structure or content" under section 4.1 of the terms?
> 2. Storing chapters on a device. The terms allow offline storage with a refresh every 30 days. The Express Licensing FAQ says caching is allowed and recommended, and the developer FAQ asks for fewer than 500 consecutive verses. May the app keep a whole Bible on the user's device for offline reading if it refreshes each chapter within 30 days? If not, what is the limit in verses or chapters, and does it differ between the NASB 1995, the NLT and the NIV?
> 3. Caching on our server. May our own server keep the chapters it fetched and serve them to our app, with the same 30-day refresh, so that each reader's request does not call your API?
>
> We will follow whatever limits you give us. A written answer lets us build them into the app.
>
> Thank you,
> [name], [role], [organization]

## To the Lockman Foundation

Send through the contact on Lockman's [permissions page](https://www.lockman.org/permission-to-quote-copyright-trademark-information/).

> Subject: License for NASB text files with Strong's numbers, for a free app
>
> Hello,
>
> We are [organization], a registered organization. We are building a free, non-commercial Bible reading app for English readers. Today it shows the NASB 1995 through API.Bible, whose responses carry no Strong's numbers. We would like a reader to tap an English word and see the Hebrew or Greek word it translates, and that needs a Strong's number on each word of the NASB.
>
> 1. Do you license NASB 1995 text files with a Strong's number on each word to a free app? Is the NASB 2020 available the same way?
> 2. Which file format would we receive (USFM, USX, OSIS or another), and does it mark the words the translators supplied, which print in italics?
> 3. What are the terms: the fee, the attribution text, and whether the app may keep the whole Bible on the user's device for offline reading and search?
> 4. Your permissions page allows up to 1,000 verses stored in an electronic retrieval system without written permission. Does that limit apply to chapters an app receives under API.Bible's license and keeps on a device for offline reading?
>
> Thank you,
> [name], [role], [organization]
